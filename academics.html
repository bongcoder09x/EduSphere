Academics

Programmes, departments and how the year is structured

Six departments run undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across engineering, science and management. Every programme carries laboratory or studio work from the first semester.

Programmes

Undergraduate · 4 years

B.Tech — Computer Science & Engineering

120 seats

Algorithms, systems and applied machine learning, with electives in distributed systems and computer vision.

Undergraduate · 4 years

B.Tech — Electronics & Communication

90 seats

Signals, embedded design and VLSI, taught alongside a working fabrication and instrumentation lab.

Undergraduate · 4 years

B.Tech — Mechanical Engineering

90 seats

Undergraduate · 4 years

B.Tech — Civil Engineering

60 seats

Structures, geotechnics and transportation, including surveying fieldwork each semester.

MBA — Business Administration

60 seats

Finance, operations and marketing, with a live consulting project in the final trimester.

B.Sc — Data Science
