About

A college built around teaching, not enrolment numbers

Founded in 1998, EduSphere is an autonomous institute in Kolkata offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across engineering, science and management.

Where we came from

The institute opened with two programmes and eighty students in a rented building on the eastern edge of the city. The first graduating batch of engineers walked out into a job market that had never heard of the college — and the alumni network that formed around them is still the reason most applicants hear about us.

Three decades later the campus covers forty-two acres, but the organising idea has not changed: a student should leave able to do something, not merely able to describe it.

How we teach

Lectures are shared across a cohort, but tutorials are capped at twenty-five. Every programme carries a laboratory or studio component from the first semester, and project work is assessed by demonstration rather than by report alone.

The academic year runs two semesters with a summer term for internships, backlog clearance and elective intensives.

Accreditation

Programmes are approved by the All India Council for Technical Education and affiliated to the state technical university. Institutional accreditation is renewed on a five-year cycle.
