Admissions

Applying for the 2027 intake

Applications for all undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are open until 31 January 2027. Here is the process, start to finish.

01

Check eligibility

Undergraduate applicants need 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics; postgraduate applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree with 50 per cent or above.

02

Apply online

Complete the application form, upload mark sheets and a photograph, and pay the application fee.

03

Entrance and interview

Engineering seats follow state counselling or the institute entrance test; management applicants attend a short interview.

04

Confirm your seat

Accept the offer, pay the admission fee and complete document verification at the admissions office.

Fees and financial aid

Tuition is charged per semester and published in the admissions brochure. Merit scholarships cover up to fifty per cent of tuition for the top decile of each incoming batch, and need-based concessions are assessed separately.
