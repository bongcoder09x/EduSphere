About the institute

Founded in 1998, EduSphere is an autonomous institute in Kolkata offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across engineering, science and management.

Where we came from

The institute opened with two programmes and eighty students in a rented building on the eastern edge of the city. Three decades later the campus covers forty-two acres — but the organising idea has not changed: a student should leave able to do something, not merely able to describe it.

Vision

To be a technical institute whose graduates are trusted with work that matters, in industry and in public service.

Mission

Teach through laboratories and demonstration, keep tutorials small, and hold the curriculum to what practitioners actually use.

Campus

Forty-two acres in Salt Lake: four academic blocks, central library, two hostels, workshop and sports ground. Twenty minutes from the Sector V business district.
