Campus life and student services

Library, hostel, clubs and scholarships — and the portal where all of it is managed.

Central library

Ninety thousand volumes, open-access journals, reading rooms open until 10 pm.

Hostel

Separate men's and women's hostels, 1,200 beds, mess and laundry on campus.

Clubs and societies

Robotics, debate, photography, music and a coding club running weekend workshops.

Scholarships

Merit scholarships cover up to fifty per cent of tuition for the top decile of each batch.

Sports

Cricket, football and athletics facilities, plus indoor courts for badminton and table tennis.

Health and wellbeing

A campus clinic with a resident nurse and weekly counselling sessions.
