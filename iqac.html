About IQAC

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell plans and monitors quality initiatives across teaching, research and administration. It meets quarterly and publishes its minutes to the institute community.

NAAC

Institutional accreditation is renewed on a five-year cycle. The most recent peer team visit is scheduled for March 2027; the SSR and supporting documents are available from the IQAC office.
