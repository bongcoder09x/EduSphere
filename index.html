Why here

Three things we do differently

Teaching that scales

Small tutorial groups alongside large lectures, so a first-year student is never one of four hundred faces in a hall.

Labs, not slideshows

Every programme carries a laboratory or studio component from the first semester, with equipment access outside class hours.

Placement from year three

A dedicated careers office runs internships, mock interviews and on-campus recruitment through the final two years.
