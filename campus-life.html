Campus life

Forty-two acres, and something happening on most of them

Library, hostels, sports facilities and student-run clubs — the parts of college that never make it onto a syllabus.

Central library

Ninety thousand volumes, open-access journals and quiet reading rooms open until 10 pm.

Hostels

Separate men's and women's hostels on campus, 1,200 beds, with mess and laundry.

Sports ground

Cricket, football and athletics facilities, plus indoor courts for badminton and table tennis.

Student clubs

Robotics, debate, photography, music and an active coding club that runs weekend workshops.

A week on campus

Classes run from 9 am to 4 pm, with laboratories and the library open well past that. Club activity clusters on Wednesday afternoons, when no lectures are scheduled.

  • Monday — department seminars
  • Wednesday — club afternoon, no lectures
  • Friday — sports league fixtures
  • Saturday — campus tours for prospective students
